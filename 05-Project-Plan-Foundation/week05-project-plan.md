# Project Plan Foundation: RCHN Patient Intake and Scheduling System
**Consultant:** Andres Macias
**Role:** Associate Consultant, Project Manager, NovaTech Consulting
**Engagement:** Riverside Community Health Network (RCHN)
**Date:** September 24, 2026
---
## Scope
### In Scope, Phase One
Patient intake workflow standardization across all six clinics 
Appointment scheduling system replacement 
Staff training for the new system at all six locations 
Initial post-launch rollout support

### Out of Scope, Phase One
Patient-facing online scheduling portal
Billing system integration with the new scheduling data 
Executive reporting dashboard for clinic leadership 
Mobile application for clinic staff 

### Scope Justification
Mobile application for clinic staff, this out of scope should be excluded from phase one because it creates a distraction for scope, schedule, cost, resources, and brings a new level of complexity to the project when it isn’t necessary for phrase one in the project.

---
## SMART Objectives
### Objective 1
By the end of the project, The RCHN intake and scheduling system workflow will be replaced in all six clinics.
**Connection to Product Vision:** For RCHN Clinic Intake Coordinator who needs an updated intake and scheduling a unified system, will provide a better workflow across all six clinics.

### Objective 2
By the end of the project, The RCHN Clinic Intake Coordinator will complete a single patient registration using a new system with faster accessibility and less screens.
**Connection to Product Vision:** For RCHN Clinic Intake Coordinator who the current system requires too many screens to complete a single patient registration, a unified system will provide a better workflow for intake and scheduling across the six clinics.

---
## Planning Constraints
### Constraint 1
Resource Restrictions: Lack of Staff Training
**Effect on Planning:** The lack of staff training will affect the planning delaying the smart objective because NovaTech team will have to hire external consultants to train the Clinic staff across all six clinics.

### Constraint 2
Scope Restrictions: Budget Cap
**Effect on Planning:** The financial limitations will affect the planning since NovaTech will not be able to buy or build software for the six clinics, forcing the staff to learn to adapt to existing software due to budget constraints.

---
## Assumptions
Assumption 1: NovaTech assumes that the intake and scheduling system will be replaced across all of the six clinics.  

If this goes wrong, the intake and scheduling system will remain inadequate for all six clinics involved. The NovaTech will have to hire extra consultants to provide training to the clinics’ staff. 

Assumption 2: NovaTech assumes that a single patient registration will be faster and have less screens. 

If this goes wrong, the RCHN patient registration process will be inadequate, and the staff will have to learn to adapt to the current software.
