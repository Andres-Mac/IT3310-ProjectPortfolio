# User Persona and Product Vision: RCHN Patient Intake and Scheduling System
**Consultant:** Andres Macias
**Role:** Associate Consultant, Product Manager, NovaTech Consulting
**Engagement:** Riverside Community Health Network (RCHN)
**Date:** September 16, 2026
---
## User Persona
**Name:** Terry
**Role:** Intake Coordinator
**Years in Role:** 3-7 Years
**Tech Comfort Level:** High,learns new software quickly
---
### Primary Work Goal
The intake coordinator's main goal is to provide an efficient intake and scheduling service for patients' needs across all six clinics. 
---
### Main Frustration with the Current System
Intake coordinators report that the current system requires too many screens to complete a single patient's registration. This affects the intake and scheduling process across the six clinics and leads to inefficient customer service and frustration among the staff.
---
### What Would Make Their Work Better
A more unified system will provide a better workflow for intake and scheduling across the six clinics.
---
## Product Vision Statement
For Clinic Intake Coordinator who the current system requires too many screens to complete a single patient registration, a unified system will provide a better workflow for intake and scheduling across the six clinics.
