## Agile Delivery Fit
The parts that will benefit from the iterative delivery are the patients’ intake forms, and the scheduling workflows across the six clinics. By breaking down the project intake and scheduling system need into smaller pieces, and by creating a sprint goal the scrum team will gain increments for the project. The need for feedback is necessary as the iterative approach indicates that delivering the work into small pieces, Novatech will identify how staff will react and adapt to the new systems intended to be changed. It also creates a conversation among stakeholders and provides a new implementation approach as they move along with new systems. During the process of learning and adaptation, the staff can get trained and learn from the newly implemented software. As the sprint goal moves along, one project area that may still require strong advance would be transferring data into the new system. However, this should be planned ahead of time with stakeholders.
