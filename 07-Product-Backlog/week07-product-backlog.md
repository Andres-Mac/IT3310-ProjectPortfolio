## Product Backlog

### Story 1
**User Story:** As a Clinic Intake Coordinator, I want a standardized RCHN intake form for all patients across six clinics, so that I can access RCHN patients' medical information accurately to distinguish each patient's information across six clinics. 
**Acceptance Criteria:** Given the Clinic Intake Coordinator accesses the intake system, when: they open the RCHN intake standardized form for any of the six clinics. Then,  the standardized forms and personal information must be identical across all six clinics. 
**Story Points:** 5
**MoSCoW Priority:** Must Have

### Story 2
**User Story:** As a Clinic Manager, I want view appointment availability across the six clinics, So I can identify and managing intake scheduling across six clinics, so that I can identify and managing intake scheduling across six clinics.
**Acceptance Criteria:** Given the Clinic Manager identifies a scheduling discrepancy, when they select an available appointment block on the cross-clinic calendar. Then, it must immediately be open the scheduling or intake screen for that clinic.
**Story Points:** 8
**MoSCoW Priority:** Must Have

### Story 3
**User Story:** As a Clinic Manager, I want to report cross-clinic wait times, so that I can compare the performance of each clinic to prevent delays and isolated operational failure.
**Acceptance Criteria:** Given multiple clinics are dealing with varying patient volumes or traffic. When comparing wait times. Then, the dashboard must rank clinics from highest to lowest wait times using color codes to alert the six clinics.
**Story Points:** 13
**MoSCoW Priority:** Must Have

### Story 4
**User Story:** As a Front Desk Staff, I want send appointments reminders to RCHN patients, so that I can notify and verify the patients' clinical medical care.  
**Acceptance Criteria:** Given Front desk staff manage bookings for multiple sister locations, when a notification is automated generated.  
Then, the system must automatically insert the exact clinic name, physical address, and specific check-in instructions for the corresponding clinic out of the six.
**Story Points:** 8
**MoSCoW Priority:** Must Have

### Story 5
**User Story:** As a Intake Specialist, I want update patient contact information correctly, so that I can maintain and prevent any patient’s identification of mismatching or duplicates within the patient’s registry across all six clinics.
**Acceptance Criteria:** Given the Intake Specialist  is modifying a patient's contact details,  when the entered patients’ phone number or email matches an existing, different patient record within the network database across six clinics. Then the system must flag any warnings to prevent accidental identity blending or duplicate profiles.
**Story Points:** 5
**MoSCoW Priority:** Should Have

## Prioritization Rationale
### Must Have Justification
I chose this backlog: standardized RCHN intake form for all patients across six clinics.  By implementing a standardized intake form in the current planning horizon, we believe this is important so we can  achieve our goal across the six clinics. We want to prevent duplicates from blending medical records and data into our systems.  As per our scope, we need to create a unified system using the same data entries in the form so the same intake form can be used across the six clinics. The delivery condition will ensure a patient can visit an of the six clinic locations and keep their information safe at the same time.
---
### Won't Have Rationale
 I chose this backlog: Customize dashboard colors were excluded for now.  I deferred from my planning horizon because in my opinion the functional value of this item can wait so RCHN should prioritize the form standardization instead. We can use other layouts to inform and avoid delays as we might consider this to wait for our next phase in the project.
